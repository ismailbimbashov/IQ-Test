How it's scored

Hard questions count three times as much as easy ones, medium twice. Time left on the clock adds up to 10% to your weighted accuracy. That figure is compared with the norm for your age group and converted to a score with mean 100 and standard deviation 15, reported between 70 and 160.

You can skip, go back, and flag questions. Progress is saved in this browser, so a refresh won't lose your answers. The test submits itself at 00:00.

This is a practice assessment with illustrative norms, not a clinically standardised test. Treat the score as an estimate.
